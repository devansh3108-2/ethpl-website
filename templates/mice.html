What MICE covers

Four kinds of event, four different problems

They are grouped under one acronym, but they are not the same job. Each has its own constraints, and we plan each differently.

Meetings

Offsites, sales kick-offs, leadership retreats, training weeks and annual general meetings. Smaller groups, short notice, and a schedule that has to hold — the work is logistics precision, not spectacle.

Incentives

Reward travel for top performers, dealers and channel partners. Corporate-funded but leisure in feel — the itinerary has to earn its place as a prize, which makes destination and experience selection the whole job.

Conferences

Annual summits, dealer meets and industry conventions. Pillarless hall capacity, breakout rooms, staging and hybrid streaming decide the shortlist long before anything else does.

Exhibitions

Trade shows and expos, where we handle the delegation rather than the show floor: travel, accommodation near the venue, transfers around exhibition hours, and documentation for equipment and staff.

Working with us

How a corporate event comes together

Group travel does not book itself from a search box. It is a proposal cycle, and this is what it looks like.

1

Brief

Delegate count, dates, budget range and what the event has to achieve. Rough numbers are enough to start — we will tell you what actually drives the cost.

2

Proposal

A costed plan: shortlisted venues, indicative airfares, a day-by-day outline and a per-delegate breakdown, so you can take a real number to your approver.

3

Lock & prepare

Room blocks and airline seats held, visas filed, rooming lists and manifests built, production and transport briefed against the final schedule.

4

On the ground

Our team travels with the group — airport desk, hotel check-in, session timings and transfers — followed by one consolidated invoice on return.
